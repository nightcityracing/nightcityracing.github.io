1. Overview & Data Minimization

Night City Racing (operated as an open web portal on nightcityracing.github.io) is designed with a strict "privacy-by-default" philosophy. We operate purely as an unblocked casual gaming website. You can explore, discover, and play every single game in our library without ever creating an account, sharing your name, or providing an email address.

Zero Personal Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or behavioral advertising networks under any circumstances.

2. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

We take the protection of minors and young learners extremely seriously. In compliance with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect, retain, or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not feature user profiles, social forums, public chat boards, or direct messaging systems where minors could unintentionally disclose identifying information.
  • If a parent or school guardian believes that a minor has provided us with contact information through our contact form, please notify us immediately at privacy@nightcityracing.github.io, and we will purge the correspondence immediately.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit Night City Racing, standard server log information is processed by GitHub Pages (our hosting provider). This may include:

  • Technical Logs: Anonymized IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, device operating system, and referral headers used solely to diagnose server errors and mitigate DDoS attacks.
  • Diagnostic Performance Metrics: Aggregate page load times and HTTP status codes to ensure high uptime.

4. Cookies & HTML5 LocalStorage

Night City Racing itself does not use tracking cookies for behavioral profiling. However:

  • Game Progress & High Scores: Individual HTML5 games use browser localStorage or IndexedDB locally on your device to save your level progression, unlocked cars, and high scores. This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to our servers.
  • Clearing Progress: You can wipe all game saves at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies.

5. Third-Party Game Embeds & Iframes

Certain titles in our catalog are hosted on dedicated gaming CDNs or open-source GitHub Pages mirrors. While our QA team inspects each iframe before listing to ensure there are no malicious scripts or predatory redirects, these third parties operate under their respective privacy terms.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you possess statutory data protection rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: Request disclosure of any personal data processed about you.
  • Right to Deletion: Request the deletion of any communications or support tickets submitted by you.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of whether you exercise privacy rights.

7. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy, contact our designated privacy compliance officer:

Email: privacy@nightcityracing.github.io
Subject: Privacy & Data Inquiry
Response Window: Within 48 business hours.
