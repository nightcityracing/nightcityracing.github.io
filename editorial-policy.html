1. Core Editorial Principles

Our mission is to safeguard the joy of instant web gaming. To achieve this, our editorial board follows four steadfast principles:

  • Player-First Experience: Zero interstitials, no misleading "Download" or "Update Flash" buttons, and no audio autoplay that disrupts classroom or office settings.
  • Performance Benchmark Integrity: Games must maintain at least 50 FPS on modest integrated GPUs (e.g. Intel HD 620 or equivalent ARM Chromebook processors).
  • Open Web Technologies: We exclusively curate HTML5, WebGL, and WebAssembly applications that run natively in modern evergreen browsers without external plugins.
  • Zero Predatory Monetization: No pay-to-win locks, real-money gambling mechanics, or deceptive micro-transactions.

2. Technical Benchmarking Process

Before a title receives approval from our editorial desk, it is deployed onto our internal test harness and evaluated across three distinct device profiles:

A

Entry-Level School Chromebook (Intel Celeron / 4GB RAM)

We verify boot time (under 4 seconds on broadband), memory consumption (under 450MB Heap), and smooth keyboard mapping without stutter.

B

Mid-Range Desktop / Laptop (Windows 11 / macOS / Linux)

Tested for full 60 FPS V-Sync stability, high-DPI scaling, and responsive fullscreen toggle with the Esc/F key shortcuts.

C

Touch-Enabled Tablets & Mobile (iPadOS / Android)

Evaluated for virtual gamepad overlays, viewport meta responsiveness, and touch gesture handling.

3. Child Safety & COPPA Compliance

A substantial portion of our visitors are students playing during study breaks or free periods. Consequently, content safety is paramount:

Strict Content Restrictions: We prohibit games featuring realistic gore, graphic violence, substance abuse, gambling mechanisms (such as simulated casino slots or loot boxes), or predatory links directing to off-site chat rooms.

We do not collect personal identifying information (PII) from children under 13, and our servers do not log student browsing profiles.

4. Editorial Independence & Ethics

Night City Racing does not sell positions on our homepage, category grids, or "Hot Games" lists. Placement is determined purely by popularity, player feedback, and technical performance. If a game fails to meet our standards, it is rejected regardless of financial sponsorship proposals.

5. Broken Link Policy & Continuous Monitoring

External CDNs, hosting services, and third-party mirrors can occasionally fail. We operate automated daily ping checks against all game URLs. If a game iframe throws a 404, CORS block, or infinite loader:

  1. The broken link is flagged automatically in our triage queue.
  2. An engineer verifies whether an alternate clean mirror is available within 24 hours.
  3. If no functional unblocked version exists, the game is temporarily delisted to avoid frustrating players.

6. Developer Submissions

Are you an indie game developer looking for hundreds of thousands of passionate players? We actively welcome game submissions! Please review our criteria before reaching out:

  • Must be packaged as HTML5 / WebGL (self-hosted iframe or clean GitHub Pages / itch.io embed).
  • Zero intrusive unskippable external video ads or popup redirects.
  • Keyboard controls documented clearly on the opening title screen.

Submit your title to devs@nightcityracing.github.io with a playable demo link.
