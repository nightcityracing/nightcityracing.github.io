Magic Tiles

About Magic Tiles

Magic Tiles is the hit musical rhythm arcade game. Tap black piano tiles as they stream down the screen in sync with world-famous classical and pop songs.

Game Features

Huge Song Library

Play classical masterpieces, EDM tracks, and pop hits

Rhythm Precision

Hit long notes, double taps, and fast rapid runs

Speed Challenge

Song tempo accelerates as you progress through each track

How to Play

Mouse Click / 1 2 3 4 Keys

Tap Piano Tiles

Pro Tips:

  • Listen closely to the song rhythm to anticipate tile arrival times.
  • Hold long tiles all the way until the end of the tail for bonus points.
  • Never click on white tiles or blank areas, as it causes an instant fail.
