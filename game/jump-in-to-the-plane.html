Jump In To The Plane

About Jump In To The Plane

Jump In To The Plane features wild stunt launching where your goal is to launch a car off massive ramps and land straight into the open cargo bay of a flying airplane!

Game Features

Stunt Plane Targets

Land your stunt vehicle into moving cargo aircraft

Rocket Engine Boost

Attach giant jet engines to propel your car

Crazy Upgrades

Upgrade Ramp Speed, Jet Engine, and Bonus Earnings

How to Play

Space / Click

Accelerate Down Ramp

Shift

Activate Jet Booster

Left Right / A D

Mid-Air Pitch Control

Pro Tips:

  • Upgrade Jet Engine power to gain horizontal velocity in mid-air.
  • Adjust car pitch in flight so your hood points directly at the plane bay.
  • Fire jet boost right as you leave the ramp edge for maximum lift.
