Rail Surfers

About Rail Surfers

Rail Surfers is an adrenaline-pumping subway parkour race! Sprint along railway tracks, surf on hoverboards, leap over obstacles, and escape the grumpy inspector.

Game Features

High-Speed Running

Dodge oncoming trains, roadblocks, and light poles

Hoverboard Thrills

Double-click to activate crash-resistant hoverboards

Athletic Power-ups

Super sneakers, coin magnets, and rocket jetpacks

How to Play

WASD / Arrow Keys

Switch Lanes, Jump, Slide

Spacebar

Activate Hoverboard

Pro Tips:

  • Equip hoverboards whenever approaching congested train corridors.
  • Swipe down in mid-air to immediately cancel jumps and land safely.
  • Collect daily letters to complete challenge words for bonus coin boxes.
