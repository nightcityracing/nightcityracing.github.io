About Rally Racer Dirt

Rally Racer Dirt is a realistic drift-based rally racing game. Master side-ways dirt drifting across gravel, mud, and tarmac tracks with authentic rally vehicles.

Game Features

Realistic Dirt Drift

Authentic rally car drifting and slide physics

Custom Rally Cars

Tuned rally cars built for dirt and gravel roads

Real-Time Counter

Earn points for drift duration and slide angle

How to Play

Up / W

Accelerate

Down / S

Brake

Space

Handbrake Drift

Pro Tips:

  • Flick the steering wheel away from the turn then quickly into it to initiate drift.
  • Maintain partial throttle during dirt drifts to keep momentum going.
  • Adjust suspension stiffness for gravel vs tarmac tracks.
