Construction Ramp Jumping

About Construction Ramp Jumping

Construction Ramp Jumping lets you launch heavy construction machinery off colossal ramps! Boost your engine power and smash through obstacles for maximum distance.

Game Features

Heavy Machinery

Drive excavators, dump trucks, and bulldozers

Colossal Ramps

Launch vehicles high into the sky off giant jump ramps

Destruction Rewards

Earn points for destroying buildings and targets

How to Play

Space / Click

Accelerate Down Ramp

Mouse Drag

Aim / Adjust Angle

Shift

Rocket Booster

Pro Tips:

  • Upgrade Rocket Boost first to gain maximum flight distance.
  • Time your boost right at the tip of the ramp for optimal angle.
  • Smash through high-value targets downfield for extra bonus points.
