Snowboard Kings 2022

About Snowboard Kings 2022

Snowboard Kings 2022 lets you conquer legendary snowy peaks. Perform aerial spins, weave through pine forests, and race toward mountain resort cabins.

Game Features

Classic Snowboarding

Smooth alpine carving controls and arcade downhill racing

Winter Hazards

Dense pine trees, ice patches, cliff drops, and snowmobiles

Rider Progression

Unlock pro snowboarders and high-performance snowboards

How to Play

Arrow Keys / WASD

Steer and Carve

Spacebar

Speed Boost

Pro Tips:

  • Carve smoothly from side to side to maintain balance on icy slopes.
  • Stay clear of snow drifts that drastically reduce forward momentum.
  • Hit jump kickers at full speed to perform aerial stunt tricks.
