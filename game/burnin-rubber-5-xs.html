Burnin Rubber 5 Xs

About Burnin Rubber 5 XS

Burnin Rubber 5 XS is an explosive combat racing game where heavy weaponry meets blazing speed. Equip your ride with machine guns and rockets to obliterate rival racers.

Game Features

Heavy Arsenal

Equip homing rockets, miniguns, and landmines

Combat Arenas

Race through urban zone battlegrounds

Vehicle Destruction

Smash and blow up opponent cars

How to Play

WASD / Arrows

Drive Vehicle

Shift / X

Fire Primary Weapon

Space / Z

Fire Secondary Weapon

Pro Tips:

  • Save homing rockets for opponents ahead on straight tracks.
  • Drop landmines right before tight corners to block chasers.
  • Collect health and ammo crates scattered along the track.
