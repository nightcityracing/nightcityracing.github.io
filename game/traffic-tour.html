About Traffic Tour

Traffic Tour is a premium highway racing game featuring realistic graphics, smooth handling, multiple weather conditions, 100+ online missions, and real-time multiplayer.

Game Features

Real-Time Multiplayer

Compete against real players online in head-to-head races

100+ Missions

Complete career challenges and highway overtake goals

Realistic Environments

Drive in Highway, City, Desert, Rain, and Night modes

How to Play

Up / W

Accelerate

Down / S

Brake

Nitro Boost

Pro Tips:

  • Overtake traffic closely at speeds above 100 km/h for nitro replenishment.
  • Switch to hood or cockpit camera for better precision in dense traffic.
  • Upgrade your car's handling stat first to make high-speed dodging easier.
