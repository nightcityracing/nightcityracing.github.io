Kart Race 3D

About Kart Race 3D

Kart Race 3D is a fun, fast-paced kart racing game filled with power-up items, drift boosts, and colorful tracks. Outsmart opponents with missiles and speed mushrooms!

Game Features

Combat Power-Ups

Use rockets, oil slick traps, and turbo mushrooms

Drift Boost Mechanic

Power-slide around corners to charge mini-turbo boosts

Vibrant Circuits

Race across beach resorts, candy tracks, and castles

How to Play

WASD / Arrows

Steer & Drive Kart

Space

Drift / Hop

Shift / E

Use Power-Up Item

Pro Tips:

  • Hold drift through long turns until your tires spark blue for a mini-turbo.
  • Hold green shell power-ups behind your kart to shield against incoming rockets.
  • Use turbo mushrooms when crossing off-road grass shortcuts.
