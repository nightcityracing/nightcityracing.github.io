About Hill Climb Pixel Car

Hill Climb Pixel Car features charm-filled 2D retro pixel physics racing. Balance your vehicle over steep hills, collect coins, and upgrade engine, suspension, and tires.

Game Features

Retro Pixel Graphics

Nostalgic 2D pixel art style with smooth physics

Challenging Hills

Navigate steep inclines, bumps, and rocky gaps

Vehicle Upgrades

Tune Engine, Suspension, Tires, and 4WD

How to Play

Right / D

Gas / Tilt Forward

Left / A

Brake / Tilt Back

Space

Nitro (If Equipped)

Pro Tips:

  • Feather the gas on steep hills to keep your front wheels grounded.
  • Tilt backward in mid-air to ensure a flat, safe landing.
  • Upgrade Suspension first to handle rough rocky terrain cleanly.
