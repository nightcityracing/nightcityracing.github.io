Melon Playground

About Melon Playground

Melon Playground is a creative 2D physics sandbox. Spawn ragdoll characters, place weapons, build contraptions, and watch chaotic physics unfold.

Game Features

Sandbox Freedom

Spawn melon, pumpkin, and apple ragdoll characters

Huge Arsenal

Firearms, blades, syringes, vehicles, and explosives

Contraption Building

Connect objects with ropes, wires, and mechanical joints

How to Play

Mouse Drag / Drop

Spawn & Move Objects

Right Click

Context Menu / Freeze / Activate

Pro Tips:

  • Freeze ragdolls in mid-air to construct elaborate cinematic scenes.
  • Experiment with different syringe chemicals to trigger wild mutations.
  • Use cables and pistons to build functional catapults and vehicles.
