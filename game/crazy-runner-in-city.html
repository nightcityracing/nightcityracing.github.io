About Crazy Runner in City

Crazy Runner in City sends you sprinting across bustling metropolitan streets. Leap over road barriers, slide under trucks, and collect gold coins.

Game Features

Urban Parkour

Sprint across busy roads, sidewalks, and subway lines

Traffic Hazards

Dodge speeding buses, construction cones, and roadblocks

Power-up Boosters

Grab coin magnets, super sneakers, and score multipliers

How to Play

WASD / Arrows

Lane Switch, Jump, Slide

Pro Tips:

  • Jump and immediately slide to recover rapidly from high jumps.
  • Prioritize grabbing the coin magnet for effortless currency collection.
  • Stay in the center lane whenever possible for maximum reaction flexibility.
