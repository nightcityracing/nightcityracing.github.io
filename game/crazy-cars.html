Crazy Cars

About Crazy Cars

Crazy Cars is a vibrant 3D arcade stunt racing game packed with loops, giant ramps, and hilarious physics. Drive through obstacle playgrounds and collect stars!

Game Features

Stunt Playgrounds

Tracks filled with loop-de-loops, ramps, and obstacles

Fun Physics

Bouncy car suspension and wild acrobatic jumps

Collectible Stars

Find hidden stars across every arena to unlock new cars

How to Play

WASD / Arrows

Drive Vehicle

Space

Jump / Air Spin

Shift

Nitro Boost

Pro Tips:

  • Press Jump right as you hit ramps to reach high floating stars.
  • Use Nitro mid-loop to avoid falling off upside-down track loops.
  • Explore off-road secrets in each playground for bonus collectibles.
