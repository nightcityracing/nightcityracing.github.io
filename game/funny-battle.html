Funny Battle

About Funny Battle

Funny Battle is a hilarious ragdoll combat simulator. Place wacky soldiers, archers, and heavy artillery on the battlefield and watch chaotic fights unfold.

Game Features

Ragdoll Physics

Comedic wobbly animations and over-the-top physics knockouts

Tactical Unit Placement

Plan formations and balance unit point costs against rivals

Bizarre Units

Knights, cavemen, bazooka troopers, and giant bosses

How to Play

Mouse Left Click

Place Units

Mouse Right Click

Remove Units

WASD / Mouse Drag

Move Camera

Pro Tips:

  • Place sturdy frontline melee units to absorb enemy ranged fire.
  • Position snipers and archers on elevated ridges for superior line of sight.
  • Counter swarms of cheap units with wide-area explosive artillery.
