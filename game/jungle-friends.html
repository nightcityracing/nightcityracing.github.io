About Jungle Friends

Jungle Friends is an enchanting farm and sanctuary management puzzle. Grow crops, feed cute exotic animals, and expand your thriving jungle paradise.

Game Features

Animal Sanctuary

Adopt foxes, monkeys, tigers, and pandas

Resource Harvesting

Grow fruit trees, harvest berries, and deliver food

Sanctuary Upgrades

Expand new territory plots and build animal habitats

How to Play

WASD / Arrows / Mouse

Move Caretaker

Pro Tips:

  • Collect berries in bulk before running rounds to feed all animals.
  • Invest early profits into movement speed and storage capacity upgrades.
  • Unlock new animal zones as soon as your budget allows.
