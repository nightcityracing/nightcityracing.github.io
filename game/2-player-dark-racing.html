About 2 Player Dark Racing

2 Player Dark Racing is an intense 2-player car racing game set in dark, neon-lit tracks. Compete side-by-side with a friend on split-screen or challenge AI drivers.

Game Features

Split Screen 2P

Race head-to-head with a friend on the same screen

Neon Dark Tracks

Futuristic night tracks with vibrant dynamic lighting

Upgradable Cars

Unlock faster vehicles and customize performance

How to Play

WASD / Arrows

Drive P1 / P2

N / Shift

Nitro Boost

Space

Handbrake

Pro Tips:

  • Use nitro on straight sections for maximum acceleration.
  • Draft behind your friend's car to gain slipstream speed.
  • Master tight drifting on dark corners to maintain speed.
