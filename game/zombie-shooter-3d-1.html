About Zombie Shooter 3D

Zombie Shooter 3D plunges you into an infected urban metropolis. Arm yourself with modern assault weapons to hold off waves of flesh-eating undead monsters.

Game Features

Third-Person Survival

Dynamic third-person combat through dark ruined streets

Deadly Undead Types

Fast runners, bloated spitters, and massive zombie brutes

Upgradable Armory

Pistols, SMGs, assault rifles, shotguns, and grenades

Pro Tips:

  • Dodge-roll to evade jumping zombies and acid spit pools.
  • Aim for headshots to conserve precious rifle ammunition.
  • Lead mutant brutes near red fuel barrels and detonate them.
