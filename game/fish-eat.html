Fish Eat Fish

About Fish Eat Fish

Dive deep into the ocean in Fish Eat Fish! Gobble up smaller marine creatures to grow into a massive apex predator while evading larger sharks.

Game Features

Underwater Growth

Eat creatures with smaller numbers to increase your size

Multiplayer Mode

Play with up to 3 players simultaneously on one keyboard

Vibrant Reefs

Vibrant undersea coral reefs filled with diverse marine fauna

How to Play

WASD / Arrows / Mouse

Swim in Direction

Pro Tips:

  • Compare numbers carefully before charging toward another fish.
  • Stay near seaweed and rocky crevices to hide from oversized sharks.
  • Grab floating starfish and pearls for bonus growth points.
