Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if a game gets stuck on a black screen?

Ensure that hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings (under Settings > System > Use graphics acceleration when available) and refresh the page. Some school networks may temporarily delay loading WebGL assets.

Do games work on school Chromebooks?

Yes! Every single game in our catalogue is benchmarked specifically on standard low-power Chromebooks to guarantee smooth framerates and reliable controls without blocking.

Is Night City Racing completely free?

100% free forever. We do not require account registration, subscriptions, or credit card information.
